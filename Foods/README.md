# 1.0 Fundamentals
- Do not store dented cans or other food with damaged packaging.
- Keep stored foods cool, clean, dark, and dry. Keep them below 70 F.
- The most optimum storage for most nonfrozen foods is 35 to 40F.
- Moisture, food, oxygen and above-freezing temperatures are the key ingredients insects need to grow.
- A few bug eggs, once they hatch, can rapidly destroy a sealed container of dry food, if they have an adequate supply of oxygen and moisture.
- Sunlight also contributes to the degradation of many stored foods.
- Cooking or freeze the containmented food, don't destroy compltely the eggs but only the insects
- Do not store food containers directly on concrete floors, due to moisture wicking from the floor.

>[!IMPORTANT]
>Do not eat moldy foods or food from bulging cans, sickness or death may result.

# 1.0 Food Storage Quantities For One Average Adult Male For One Year
- [ ] Grains - 325lbs (pasta, oats, corn, rice, barley)
- [ ] Legumes - 80lbs (beans, peas, lentils, seeds)
- [ ] Milk, dairy products, and eggs. - 50lbs (nonfat dry milk keeps longer than dried whole milk)
- [ ] Meat and meat substitutes - 20lbs.
- [ ] Fruits and vegetables - 10lbs to 30lbs
- [ ] Sweeteners - 60lbs (Honey, syrups. honey is preferred for its nutritive and antibiotic values.)
- [ ] Fats, oils and shortenings 40lbs (Includes butter, margarine, powdered butter, shortening, cooking oil, nut butters.)
- [ ] Sprouting seeds and supplies - 20 to 50lbs. (Provides live foods and essential vitamins. Great for variety and nutrition. For best results, use untreated organic whole grains, beans, and seeds. Alfalfa seeds, all types of whole grains, mung beans, soybeans, lentils, and cabbage, radish, and broccoli seeds.
- [ ] Leavenings - 1lbs
- [ ] Miscellaneous foods (Includes spices, cocoa powder, seasonings sauces, condiments, vitamins, minerals, other nutritional supplements, and so on.
